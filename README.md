## 🩸 The 30-Second Story

Somewhere right now, a health worker is trying to screen a hundred kids for anemia with one finger-prick kit and a long queue.

Somewhere else, a pregnant woman skips her checkup because the nearest lab is two bus rides away.

**Anemia is one of the most common — and most quietly ignored — health issues in the world.** Not because we can't detect it. Because detecting it *at scale* has always meant needles, labs, and waiting.

**AnemiScan asks a different question:** *what if your eyelid already told the story — and your phone just needed to learn how to read it?*

---

## 👁️ How It Works

```
     📸  Snap a photo of the inner eyelid
              │
              ▼
     🧠  Gemini Vision reads pallor & color signatures
              │
              ▼
     🚦  Risk flagged — Low / Possible Anemia
              │
              ▼
     🩺  Clear next step: "Go get it confirmed"
```

No lab. No wait. No needle. Ten seconds, one camera, one honest answer: *should you go get checked?*

## ✨ Features That Matter

> Built for real people, not just a demo screen.

- 📷 **10-Second Screening** — camera to result, no friction
- 🧠 **AI That Actually Sees** — multimodal vision model reads subtle pallor patterns
- 🚦 **No Scary Numbers** — plain-language risk flags, not confusing lab jargon
- 🌍 **Designed for the Last Mile** — built with low-connectivity, low-literacy users in mind
- 🔐 **Your Face, Your Data** — nothing stored without consent
- 🩺 **Always Ends With "See a Doctor"** — a screening tool that knows its own limits

## 🤖 On Using AI (Full Transparency)

This project uses the **Gemini API** — a generative AI vision model — to analyze eyelid images for anemia-associated pallor signals. Disclosed here, disclosed in every submission document, no exceptions. Building *with* AI responsibly is part of the point.

## ⚠️ The Honest Part

> **AnemiScan is a screening aid, not a diagnosis.**
> It's built to nudge you toward a real doctor, not replace one. Every result comes with that reminder — on purpose.
