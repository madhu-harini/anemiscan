
No blood tests. No lab visits. No waiting. Just a phone camera and a few seconds.

---

## ✨ Key Features (Planned)

- 📷 **Instant Screening** — capture and analyze in under 10 seconds
- 🧠 **AI-Powered Analysis** — multimodal vision model trained to recognize pallor patterns
- 🚦 **Simple Risk Output** — clear, non-alarming "Low Risk" / "Consult a Doctor" indicators
- 🌍 **Built for Accessibility** — designed for low-resource, low-literacy, rural-first use
- 🔒 **Privacy-First** — no image storage without explicit consent
- 🩺 **Always Points to Real Care** — every result nudges users toward proper medical confirmation

## 🤖 Generative AI Disclosure

This project uses **Gemini API**, a generative AI vision model, to analyze eyelid images for pallor patterns associated with anemia risk. This use is disclosed transparently here, on the Round 1 submission, and will be detailed further in all Round 2 documentation, in compliance with Omnikon competition guidelines.

---

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

> **AnemiScan is a screening aid — not a medical diagnostic tool.**
> All results are risk indicators meant to encourage timely professional consultation, not replace clinical blood testing. Always consult a healthcare provider for confirmed diagnosis.
